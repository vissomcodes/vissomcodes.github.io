Convection lab

A small fluid solver running live in your browser, in the spirit of my thesis topic. The bottom wall is hot and the top is cold. Warm fluid rises, cool fluid sinks, and cells form. Click and drag to add heat.

cold wallhot wall
0Kinetic energy (arb. units)
0.50Mean temperature

Try buoyancy 0: heat only conducts, so there is no flow. Flip the heating (hot on top): the fluid stays stably layered and convection dies out. These are the same two quantities I track in the thesis, kinetic energy and mean temperature.

This is a teaching toy (a 64 × 64 stable-fluids solver with a simple buoyancy term), not a validated CFD result. My validated work is in the projects below.
