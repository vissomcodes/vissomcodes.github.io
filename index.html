About

CFD, Flow Physics
& High-Performance Computing

I'm a CFD-focused aerospace engineering Master's student in the Erasmus Mundus TFMASA programme — studying across France, Belgium, and Germany with a deep focus on aerodynamic and thermal-fluid simulations.

My research centres on buoyancy-driven natural-convection flows with internal heat generation, using OpenFOAM on Linux/HPC systems. Before my Master's, I spent nearly two years as a Project Engineer at S&I Engineering Solutions in India, running RANS simulations for aircraft, rockets, UAVs, and industrial configurations.

I enjoy the intersection of numerical methods, physical intuition, and engineering problem-solving — and I'm always looking for new challenges in simulation-driven engineering.

Open to relocation across Europe and international engineering opportunities.

Selected Work

01
Numerical Validation of Lid-Driven & Natural Convection Flows
CFD validation studies for lid-driven cavity and natural-convection benchmark cases in OpenFOAM. Mesh-independence studies and benchmark comparison with Botella–Peyret and De Vahl Davis reference data.
OpenFOAM Validation Thermal Flow
02
Unstart Phenomenon of a Scramjet Inlet in Hypersonic Regime
Simulated mixed-compression scramjet inlet under high-Mach conditions using RANS + SA/k–ω turbulence models. Anisotropic grid optimisation to capture shock oscillations and transient unstart behaviour.

CFD parametric study of bleed-hole configurations on shock structure and intake efficiency. Identified optimum bleed placement through sensitivity studies, mesh-independence validation, and backflow analysis.
